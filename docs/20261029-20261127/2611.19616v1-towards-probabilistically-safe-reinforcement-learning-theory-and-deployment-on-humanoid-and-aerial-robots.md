## 摘要
强化学习（RL）使机器人能够习得复杂技能，但标准的 RL 形式并不保证所学策略满足安全约束，这限制了其在安全关键场景中的应用。现有具有可证明保证的安全 RL 方法通常仅支持受限类别的约束，并且要求目标函数和约束函数的无偏估计，这阻碍了它们与近端策略优化（PPO）等最先进算法的直接集成。本文扩展了近期提出的基于鲁棒安全梯度流的强化学习（RSGF-RL）框架，以克服这些限制。我们的形式化方法适用于一般类别的轨迹级安全约束，包括以规定概率避免碰撞等机会约束，并且我们的分析允许有偏的值估计和梯度估计。我们证明，以高概率，整个训练过程中的约束违反量可由估计偏差加上一个随 rollout 数量增长而消失的项来界定，并且如果初始策略不安全，该违反量会衰减到这一水平。我们还建立了迭代点收敛到原始安全 RL 问题近似 KKT 点的性质。最后，我们展示了采用 PPO 风格估计实现的 RSGF-RL 能够在 Unitree G1 人形机器人上实现全身控制策略的零样本 sim-to-real 部署，并在 Crazyflie 无人机上实现无碰撞航点导航。

## Abstract
Reinforcement learning (RL) enables robots to acquire complex skills, but standard RL formulations do not guarantee that learned policies satisfy safety constraints, which limits their use in safety-critical settings. Existing safe RL methods with provable guarantees typically support only restricted classes of constraints and require unbiased estimators of the objective and constraint functions, which prevents their direct integration with state-of-the-art algorithms such as proximal policy optimization (PPO). In this paper, we extend the recently introduced robust safe gradient flow-based reinforcement learning (RSGF-RL) framework to overcome these limitations. Our formulation accommodates a general class of trajectory-level safety constraints, including chance constraints such as collision avoidance with a prescribed probability, and our analysis allows for biased value and gradient estimators. We show that, with high probability, the constraint violation throughout training is bounded by the estimator bias plus a term that vanishes as the number of rollouts grows, and decays to this level if the initial policy is unsafe. We also establish the convergence properties of the iterates to approximate KKT points of the original safe RL problem. Finally, we show that RSGF-RL implemented with PPO-style estimates enables zero-shot sim-to-real deployment of whole-body control policies on a Unitree G1 humanoid and collision-free waypoint navigation on a Crazyflie drone.
